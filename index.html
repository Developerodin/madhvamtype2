Questions about Madhavam

Clear answers for buyers exploring gated residential plots in Rajnagar Extension, Delhi NCR.

What is Madhavam?

Madhavam is an approved gated residential plotted development by Earth Waves Developers (Earthwaves Developers Pvt Ltd) at Rajnagar Extension, Ghaziabad, Delhi NCR. The brochure describes about 4 acres with 87 thoughtfully planned freehold, GDA-approved plots, landscaped outdoor living, and a clubhouse of about 5,500 sq. ft. UP RERA registration number UPRERAPRJ245925/09/2026; Authority website https://www.up-rera.in.

Where is Madhavam in Delhi NCR?

Madhavam is at KH. No.385M, 386M, Bhowapur, Raj Nagar Extension, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, 201017 — on the Ghaziabad side of Delhi NCR, serving buyers from Delhi, Noida, Ghaziabad, Faridabad, and Gurugram.

Who is developing Madhavam?

Madhavam is developed by Earthwaves Developers Pvt Ltd (Earth Waves Developers), GSTIN 09AADCE7736P1ZX. The corporate office is at G1, South Extension II, New Delhi. Nearby, Earthwaves delivered Radhe Radhe Aaradhayam (71 plots), about 500 metres from Madhavam — a separate project.

What amenities does Madhavam include?

Outdoor amenities include a lush green lawn with jogging track, kids play area, park gazebo, sculpture platform, badminton court, cricket net practice, yoga lawn, flower garden, and pet park. Indoor amenities in Club Madhavam (about 5,500 sq. ft.) include a multi-purpose hall with pantry, AC gymnasium, yoga and zumba room, indoor games room, and toddler play area. Common facilities include 24x7 gated security and DG power backup for common areas.
