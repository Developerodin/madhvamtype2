About Developer

Building Trust. Shaping Futures.

Earthwaves Developers Private Limited is an emerging and progressive name in the Delhi-NCR real estate landscape, driven by a management team with over 15 years of extensive experience in real estate development and the regional property market. Built on a strong understanding of land, location and evolving homebuyer aspirations, the company is committed to creating thoughtfully planned residential communities that offer a fulfilling lifestyle and enduring value.

With a vision to bring back the timeless significance of land ownership, Earthwaves is redefining residential living through its distinctive concept of “Plotted Development for Housing.” The philosophy is to give families the freedom of owning their own piece of land and the opportunity to create a home that truly reflects their lifestyle, aspirations and future.

Just 500 metres from MADHAVAM, Earthwaves successfully delivered Radhe Radhe Aaradhayam, a residential plotted development comprising 71 plots, within a span of just two years. This successful completion reflects the Group’s focus on timely execution, thoughtful planning, quality development and customer commitment, while strengthening its presence and credibility in the region.

At Earthwaves, development goes beyond creating plots and infrastructure. Every customer is considered part of the extended family, inspiring communities built on trust, transparency, quality, commitment and long-term value. With every development, Earthwaves strives to deliver on its promises and create addresses where families can grow, prosper and build lasting memories for generations.
